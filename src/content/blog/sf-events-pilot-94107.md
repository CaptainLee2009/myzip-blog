Finding out what's happening a few blocks away shouldn't take five tabs and three calendars.

We're starting a local events pilot in two San Francisco ZIP codes:
- **SoMa / Mission Bay / Potrero / Dogpatch:** [my.zip/us/94107/events](https://my.zip/us/94107/events?s=share&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=story)
- **Civic Center / Hayes Valley:** [my.zip/us/94102/events](https://my.zip/us/94102/events?s=share&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=story)

Every listing is public, ad-free, and links straight to the organizer's official RSVP page.

## Hand-curated, not scraped

We tried auto-importing calendar feeds early on. Venues broke, duplicates piled up, and off-neighborhood spam crowded out real local events.

Now we curate **Monday and Thursday drops** by hand — written in our own words, checked against the organizer's page, and paced calmly so the timeline stays readable.
